Angle guide

Panel top and phone top

Follow the arrow along each face, from the centre toward the marked top edge. The phone top is its lens end. With the device flat, measure this heading clockwise from true north. Keep the heading as you raise that edge from 0° flat to 90° vertical. At 90° the top points straight up, and the ground compass shows its heading when laid flat.

Panel front and lens azimuth

The arrows pointing out of the faces show the panel front normal and lens axis. Their horizontal projections give azimuths measured clockwise from geographic south. South is 0°, west +90°, north ±180° and east −90° or 270°. When the marked tops point east and you lift them, both faces point west (+90°). At 0° tilt the normals point straight up and their azimuths are undefined.

Phone image

The lens end points straight uphill and aligns with image top (zero additional roll). Turn the phone’s top heading to rotate the photograph. With the phone flat and its top pointing north, east appears on the left.

The phone image shows an ideal 180° fisheye. A calibrated study uses the lens and field of view of the photograph.

Sun

Solar azimuth is clockwise from true north. Elevation starts at the horizon, and zenith angle starts at straight up. Their sum is 90°. Azimuth is undefined when the sun is directly overhead or directly below. The gold line points toward the sun. Incoming sunlight travels in the opposite direction.

The example sun position is set manually. The irradiance calculation determines solar position from site, date and time.

Explore the scene

Choose Panel, Phone or Sun to see its angles. Use the arrows on a focused scene to orbit, + or − to zoom and Home to reset. Top view places north up and east right. Copy panel pose to phone aligns both illustrated top edges and tilts. Matching faces stay side by side in the same plane.
